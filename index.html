Welcome to PawCare Bookings

PawCare Bookings is a browser-based application that allows pet owners to schedule grooming appointments online without needing to call or visit in person. Choose a service, pick an available time slot, and receive an email confirmation instantly.

How Booking Works

  1. Select the grooming service your pet needs.
  2. Choose an available date and time.
  3. Fill out your contact and pet details.
  4. Receive a confirmation email with your appointment details.
  5. Get a reminder email one day before your visit.
